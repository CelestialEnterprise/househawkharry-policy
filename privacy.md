Privacy Policy – HouseHawk Harry

HouseHawk Harry is a personal real estate alert application developed by Celestial Enterprise Group LLC. The application monitors real estate listings and sends SMS notifications when properties matching predefined criteria are identified.

Information Collected
The application stores the following information:
• Phone numbers of opted-in recipients
• Property listing information used for alert notifications

Use of Information
Phone numbers are used solely for the purpose of delivering SMS alerts regarding real estate listings that match user-defined criteria.

Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. Information is used exclusively for delivering alert notifications to authorized recipients.

Message Frequency
Recipients may receive up to 2 SMS messages per day when matching properties are detected.

Opt-Out
Recipients may opt out of SMS notifications at any time by replying STOP.
